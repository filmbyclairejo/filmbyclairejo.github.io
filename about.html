hello I am clairejo and I am a film photographer and super8 videographer based in seattle, wa

I love all things film and had a passion of documenting both my life and others since 2016. Whether it is a wedding, a friend's birthday party, a weekend getaway, an engagement, or a 60th wedding anniversary, I believe the best way to capture each precious moment is through the lens of a film camera.

When I am not behind the camera, I am a nurse living in Seattle and feel extremely blessed to be able to share God’s love through each patient interaction. Outside of nursing, I love documenting God’s beauty and every sweet moment on film and Super8. Each film photo has a unique look that enhances the story behind the moment… and super8 speaks for itself.
